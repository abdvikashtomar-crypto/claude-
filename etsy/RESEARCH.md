## Findings that shaped the listings

1. **Hand embroidery is your edge, so every title says it.** Most "embroidered" tees on Etsy are machine embroidered on stock blanks at $22 to $30. Truly hand-embroidered tees sit higher: a $38 median and a top quartile of $54 in the "hand embroidered t shirt" search. Every title carries "Hand Embroidered" and every description says "not printed, not machine embroidered", which supports prices of $39 to $54.
2. **Titles lead with the phrase shoppers type.** Nobody searches for Taanka, Neelbel, Kanak or Amaltas, so those names moved into the descriptions. Titles now open with the proven phrase: "Sunflower Embroidered Shirt", "Octopus Embroidered Shirt" (the top tag, on 20 of 40 octopus results), "Wheat Embroidered Shirt", "Kantha Stitch T-Shirt", "Floral Embroidered Hat".
3. **The apple tee is a teacher gift.** On Etsy, "embroidered apple shirt" is kids' back-to-school wear. The adult demand is "apple teacher shirt": all 30 top results use it as a tag. Your copy already called it a gift for teachers, so the listing leads with that, with "teacher christmas" for the coming gift season.
4. **Seasonal timing.** It is October, so the wheat tee carries fall, harvest and Thanksgiving terms. Swap those for evergreen tags after Thanksgiving; the listing notes say which ones.
5. **Kantha has little direct competition.** Kantha results are mostly jackets and suzani coats around $60; almost nobody sells a kantha tee. The Taanka listing pairs the kantha terms with minimalist, boho and slow fashion phrases.
6. **"Blue floral embroidered shirt" means Ukrainian blouses on Etsy** (that is why the median price is $97). Neelbel therefore targets the broader "floral embroidered" phrases, with blue and black set through Etsy's color attributes.
7. **Caps:** "embroidered hat" (16 of 30 in both cap searches) and "denim hat" are the core terms, plus "floral baseball cap", "wildflower hat" and "cottagecore hat".
8. **Every tag earns its slot.** Where a first-draft tag was used by none of the 499 competitors, it was replaced with one they do use, for example "fruit embroidery" became "kindergarten teacher" (13 uses). A few zero-use tags stay on purpose because they describe what makes the product different: "back embroidery", "indian embroidery" and "hand embroidered tee".

## Changes from the Shopify copy

- **Titles** rewritten in Etsy's comma-separated keyword style, 107 to 125 of 140 characters.
- **Tags**: 13 per listing, all within Etsy's 20-character limit. Many Shopify tags could not be reused because they are too long, for example "sunflower embroidered t-shirt" (29 characters).
- **Descriptions** follow the structure your best Etsy listings already use (What it is, Details, Sizing, Made to order, Care, Why hand embroidery) as plain text, since Etsy strips HTML. The first sentence repeats the main keyword phrase, because Etsy and Google show it as the preview snippet.
- **Removed** the links to veethreads.com (Etsy does not allow pointing buyers off Etsy) and the "5,000+ Vee Threads orders" line, since those orders are not on Etsy and your Etsy shop shows 11 sales.
- **Size chart** added as the last photo on every tee.
- **Prices** in USD on your current Etsy ladder: $39 small motif, $44 to $46 medium, $54 large back piece, $32 cap.
