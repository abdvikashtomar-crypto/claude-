# Privacy Policy – Vee Threads ChatGPT Plugins

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy covers the ChatGPT plugins published by Vee Threads: **Research Papers**, **Diagrams** and **QR Code Generator**.

## What we receive
When you use a plugin, ChatGPT sends our server only the inputs needed for that request:
- **Research Papers:** your search keywords, filters and paper identifiers (DOIs or ids).
- **Diagrams:** the diagram code and title ChatGPT writes for you.
- **QR Code Generator:** the content to encode (for example a link, Wi-Fi name and password, or contact details) and your style choices.

We do not receive your ChatGPT account details, your conversation history, or your name and email address.

## How we use it
Inputs are used only to answer the request. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, build profiles, or use it to train AI models.
- Research Papers sends your keywords and identifiers to [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org), an open scholarly index, to fetch results. OpenAlex's own privacy policy applies to that request.
- Diagrams and QR codes are generated on our server and in your browser. They are not sent to third parties.

## Storage
We do not store your inputs or results. Search results from OpenAlex may be kept in server memory for up to one hour to avoid repeat lookups, and are then discarded. Our server logs record errors and basic request information (time, endpoint, status) for up to 30 days, for security and troubleshooting. We do not log the content of QR codes or diagrams.

## Cookies and tracking
The plugins use no cookies, analytics or tracking.

## Children
The plugins are not directed at children under 13.
