# Signup kit: profiles and directories

**Why these were not created automatically:** every one of these sites asks for at least one thing only you can give: a phone OTP, a CAPTCHA, a business address, a verification video, or agreement to its terms as the business owner. Accounts made by a bot from a cloud server also get flagged and suspended, and you would not own the login. So everything is prepared below; each signup should take you 3 to 10 minutes.

Use **abdvikashtomar@gmail.com** for every signup, keep one password manager entry per site, and tick the `status` column in `prospects.csv` as you go.

**Long description (about 750 characters)**
Vee Threads makes hand-embroidered clothing and accessories in a small studio in Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh. Every design is stitched by hand in real thread by women artisans who have practised embroidery for 12 to 25+ years, from fine floral detailing and name embroidery to heritage motifs and beadwork. We work on everyday pieces people actually wear: oversized cotton tees, fleece sweatshirts and hoodies, denim caps and cotton socks. Customers send us a name, a date, a pet or a sketch, and our artisans turn it into a piece that is made to order in 7 to 10 days. We also make corporate welcome kits and wedding favours in small batches. Hand embroidery takes time; one of our tees takes 24 hours of stitching. We think that is what makes it worth keeping.
