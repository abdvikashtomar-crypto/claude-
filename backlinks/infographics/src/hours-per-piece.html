Machine time vs hand time

A machine stitches a chest logo in minutes. A hand-embroidered tee takes days.

Hours of stitching for one piece. The machine row is a worked example; the hand rows are real pieces, one from our Bulandshahr studio and two timed by a maker who posted the work on Reddit.

Machine: 5,000 stitches at 1,000 stitches per minute, a common commercial speed (most commercial heads run 800 to 1,200).
