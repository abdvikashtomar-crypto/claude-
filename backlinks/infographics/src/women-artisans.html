India's craft workforce

64 out of every 100 handloom and handicraft artisans in India are women

Each square is one artisan out of 100. India has an estimated 64.66 lakh handloom and handicraft artisans, and women make up the majority of them.

Women (64) All other artisans (36)

Among handloom weavers the share is even higher: 71% are women.

Artisans are concentrated in Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Assam, Odisha, West Bengal and Tamil Nadu. Our own studio is in Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh.
