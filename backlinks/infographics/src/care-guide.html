How to wash a hand-embroidered tee so the stitches last for years

Hand stitches are anchored in the fabric, not glued on, so they do not peel. What damages them is friction, heat and twisting. Six habits prevent all three.

1. Turn it inside out

The embroidery then rubs against the drum and other clothes far less.

2. Cold or cool water

30°C or below. Hot water can shrink the cotton under the stitches and pucker the design.

3. Hand wash or a mesh bag

Hand wash is gentlest. If you use a machine, choose the delicate cycle and a laundry bag.

4. Mild detergent, no bleach

Bleach and strong detergents fade thread colours faster than the base fabric.

5. Do not wring, dry flat in shade

Press the water out with a towel and lay it flat. Skip the tumble dryer and harsh sun.

6. Iron from the back

Lay the embroidery face down on a folded towel and iron the reverse on medium heat.

Never Iron directly on the stitches, soak it overnight with other coloured clothes, or hang a wet heavy sweatshirt from the shoulders. If a thread loop snags, push it to the back with a needle. Do not cut it.
