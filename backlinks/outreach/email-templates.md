# Outreach email templates

Send from abdvikashtomar@gmail.com (or a studio address on your domain if you set one up; replies from a branded address get answered more often). Rules that matter more than the wording:

- **One email per person, written to them.** Mention the exact article you read. Generic emails get deleted.
- **Short.** Editors read on phones. Five to eight sentences.
- **One follow-up after 5 to 7 days**, then stop.
- **Never offer money for a link** and say no if they ask for payment for a "dofollow link". Paid links are what got your profile filled with spam in the first place, and Google penalises them.
- Track every send in `../prospects.csv` (status, date, reply).

Replace everything in [BRACKETS].
