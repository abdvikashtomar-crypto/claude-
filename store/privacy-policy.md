# Thread Color Finder – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

Thread Color Finder ("the extension") is published by Vee Threads (https://veethreads.com).

## What the extension does with your data

**It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

- **Colours you pick** with the eyedropper are only used to look up matching DMC thread colours inside the extension.
- **Photos you add** are processed entirely on your own computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Your saved thread list**, last colour and last-used tab are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's `storage` API. This information never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

The extension contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no remote code. It does not read the content of the web pages you visit.

## Links to our website

The extension contains links to veethreads.com. If you click one, our website's own privacy policy applies to that visit. These links carry standard "utm" campaign tags so we can see that a visit came from the extension. The tags contain no personal information.

## Permissions

- `sidePanel` – shows the extension in Chrome's side panel, so it stays open while you pick colours.
- `storage` – saves your thread list on your own device.

Questions about this policy: contact us through https://veethreads.com.
