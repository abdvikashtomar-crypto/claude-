**Language:** English

**Description**
```
Find the perfect embroidery thread for any colour, in one click.

Thread Color Finder matches any colour you see on screen, or any photo you love, to the closest DMC stranded cotton floss shades. It's built for hand embroiderers, cross-stitchers and anyone planning a stitching project.

★ PICK ANY COLOUR ON SCREEN
Use the eyedropper on any website, Pinterest board, digital pattern or photo and instantly see the 5 closest DMC thread numbers, each with a match-quality score.

★ TURN A PHOTO INTO A THREAD PALETTE
Drop, paste or upload an image and get the DMC threads you need, showing how much of the design each colour covers. Choose from 2 to 16 colours. Your image is processed on your own computer and never uploaded.

★ LOOK UP ANY DMC NUMBER
Type a number like 310, 3865 or B5200, or a name like "coral", to see the shade and find similar alternatives when your colour is out of stock.

★ BUILD A THREAD SHOPPING LIST
Save shades as you go and copy your whole list in one click before your next trip to the craft store.

WHY CRAFTERS LIKE IT
• Covers all 454 DMC stranded cotton colours, including Blanc, Ecru, White and B5200
• Uses CIEDE2000, a colour-difference formula designed to match how people actually see colour
• Opens in Chrome's side panel, so it stays open while you browse
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking

Tip: screens show colour differently from real thread, so always check your final choice against a physical DMC shade card.

ABOUT VEE THREADS
Thread Color Finder is made by Vee Threads (https://veethreads.com). We're a slow-fashion studio whose women artisans in Bulandshahr, India hand-embroider custom sweatshirts, t-shirts and caps in real thread. If you'd rather have your design stitched for you, see our custom hand embroidery: https://veethreads.com/collections/custom-hand-embroidery

DMC is a registered trademark of DMC Corporation. This extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by DMC.
```
